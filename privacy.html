Privacy Policy – Jaime YT (Gamers Connekt)

Last updated: October 23, 2026

English

Jaime YT ("the app") is used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel Gamers Connekt to upload and manage videos on that channel.

Data accessed

The app requests the following Google OAuth scopes, used only for the owner's own channel:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube – manage videos, playlists and channel metadata
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – upload videos
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl – manage videos, captions and comments over SSL
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – read channel analytics

Use, storage and sharing

Data is used only to provide the app's features for the owner's channel. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and are never sent to any server operated by the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jaime YT's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes stored credentials.

Third-party terms

By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
